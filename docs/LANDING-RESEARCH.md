# Landing-page inspiration and gallery research

Research date: 14 October 2026. Reference sites inform layout and navigation; no reference code, school photographs, branding, or unverified statistics are copied.

## Design references

- [Pinterest: Modern Kids School Landing Page, WRTeam](https://www.pinterest.com/pin/947726315350001264/): inspected the live pin's title, creator, and descriptive content in the browser. The description emphasises welcoming layouts, parent trust, and clear navigation. Screenshot capture was unavailable, so this is not represented as a full visual inspection of the pin.
- [CHES brand and digital design, Seesaw Studio](https://seesawstudio.com.au/projects/ches/): reviewed the studio case-study text and indexed visual description. Adapt the editorial hierarchy, green education palette, and distinct content panels to Brainfield's own brand.
- [The Great Hill School](https://www.thegreathillschool.com/): reviewed its public site and indexed visual description for a prominent school identity, green/white visual balance, and direct visit/admissions navigation.
- [Bedales School](https://www.bedales.org.uk/): reviewed the accessible public homepage text for school-story and admissions hierarchy; browser navigation timed out, so no successful visual inspection is claimed.

The implementation retains the founder's palette and original illustrations, adds a two-part hero feature, promotes verified recent stories, creates a dedicated results area, and gives official video content its own feature. Gallery cards use clear category/year labels, source links, and URL-persisted filters. Both campuses retain equal prominence.

## Updated achievements

This supports selection, not a claim of attendance or a competition victory. Publish as semi-finalist recognition, not the winner of that edition.
- Earlier InterswitchSPAK 3.0, physics, chemistry, and 2022 UTME records remain in the dated archive. Do not describe those as 2025 results or imply that the 3.0 champion won the newest edition.

Availability, embedding restrictions, ads, and playback remain controlled by YouTube. Browser tests verify the embed URL, accessible title, explicit playback action, and fallback link; they do not assert that third-party video playback will succeed on every network/device.

## 2025 JAMB results: unresolved source gap

Targeted searches for Brainfield Schools/College, Salolo/Abule Egba, JAMB/UTME, and 2025 did not produce a trustworthy public cohort list. The founder confirmed they do not have a source URL. Results from similarly named institutions in Port Harcourt/Owerri/Aba, unrelated admissions pages, and old UTME highlights are excluded.

The homepage and Gallery therefore have a dedicated **2025 JAMB / UTME** section with an honest pending-publication state. The 2022 score of 354 appears only in the dated historical gallery. No invented student names, scores, pass rates, result counts, or placeholder score tiles are used.

The Gallery lists all currently verified public records in `apps/web/src/content/gallery.ts`; it does not claim to contain every student's results. A complete school-approved 2025 list is still needed to fulfil the requested student-by-student results showcase. Private academic records remain within the authorised school portal design. The source list can be extended with approved public entries later without changing page layouts.
