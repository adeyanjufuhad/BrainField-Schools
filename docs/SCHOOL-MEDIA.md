Updated 15 October 2026. The founder supplied the Brainfield Schools Lagos Facebook channel and sixteen attachments. The live public page matches 39/40 Powerline Avenue, Salolo, and the supplied telephone numbers. Its photo archive includes the competition imagery and results posters supplied by the founder.

The main poster was downloaded from the full-size URL observed in the photo viewer; expiring CDN URLs are not embedded in the app. Two copies of a full JAMB slip were excluded from all repository exports because they show a registration number, date of birth, exam identifiers and scannable codes. Unrelated admissions-only images and duplicate logos are not competition evidence.

## Confirmed typed highlights

The school-published 2025 posters report **Babalola David — 364/400** (English 73, Physics 96, Mathematics 98, Chemistry 97) and **Ayomide Victoria — 364/400** (English 75, Physics 98, Mathematics 97, Chemistry 94). Each subject breakdown sums to 364. The app attributes these to the school; it does not claim to have queried JAMB accounts, independently certified a national ranking or imported a complete private cohort database.

The original posters retain further publicly shared student results. Names that cannot be read confidently are not guessed or transcribed into searchable records. Full-size links let visitors inspect the original posters. The continuation is contextualised by its paired 2025 school post. The historical 2022 poster and Jubril Dokun’s 354 are kept under 2022.

The school’s Babalola poster also reports fourth place in InterswitchSPAK 6.0 and 2024 national JETS recognition. These are explicitly school-reported claims; earlier independent coverage supports the semi-finalist stage and Sakura selection. The BOAB and EKO STEM photos are labelled archival, and the EKO scoreboard itself identifies November 2021. Mixed-school group shots are described as event participants, rather than entirely Brainfield students.
