## Handling and scope

Both originals are stored without visual retouching or embedded-metadata additions. Next.js supplies responsive, optimised browser versions. No expiring social-media URLs are embedded, and visiting the homepage does not fetch photographs from Facebook or the publishers. The public repository's code licence does not grant ownership of these photographs; third-party rights remain with their owners. These are source-attributed editorial photographs selected at the founder's request, rather than newly commissioned or school-approved campus photographs.

Images from Brainfield institutions in Port Harcourt, Aba, Owerri and Ghana were excluded. The public `brainfieldcollege.edu` Facebook profile matched Powerline Avenue but exposed only small archival campus images and a staff portrait; these were not reused. No private albums, account login, private photographs, or student records were accessed.

The InterswitchSPAK 6.0 semi-finalist card retains a competition graphic because no attributable photograph was verified for that specific record. A 3.0 winner photo must not illustrate a 6.0 semi-finalist claim. Current classroom and campus photographs still need to come from the school.
